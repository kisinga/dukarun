# Purchases

Record goods and services received from a supplier. Enter what arrived, what each item cost, and
whether you paid now, paid part of the bill, or will pay later.

## Common actions

- [Recording a credit purchase](recording-a-credit-purchase.md)
- [Creating a supplier](../suppliers/creating-a-supplier.md)

## After the purchase

If you chose **Pay later**, the unpaid amount becomes a supplier payable. When you settle it,
[record the supplier payment](../suppliers/recording-a-supplier-payment.md).
