# Receiving a customer payment

## Why this matters

Recording a payment reduces what the customer owes and keeps the customer account aligned with the
money your business actually received.

## Before you start

Open the correct customer account. Have the amount, payment method, and reference ready. If your
business uses till control, open the till that received the money. Your role must allow you to settle
customer orders.

## Steps

1. Open **Customers** and select the customer.
2. In **Customer account**, check the open invoice balance and available downpayment.
3. Enter **Payment received (KES)**.
4. Choose the **Method** and add the required reference or M-PESA details.
5. Review **How this payment will be applied**. Dukarun clears the oldest open invoices first.
6. Choose **Receive payment**. For an active M-PESA collection, the button may say
   **Send STK prompt**.
7. Confirm that **Receipt posted** appears and check the updated balance.

## What changes in Dukarun

Cash received increases and the customer's receivable falls by the amount applied to invoices. If
the payment is greater than the open invoices, the remaining amount is kept as a downpayment. The
payment does not create new revenue because the sale was recorded earlier.

## If something does not look right

- If receiving a payment is unavailable, check your permission, till status, and customer account
  integrity notice.
- Confirm the customer before posting. Payments are applied to that customer's oldest invoices.
- Do not record a new sale when collecting an existing credit balance.
