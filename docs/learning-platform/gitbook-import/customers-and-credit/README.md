# Customers & Credit

Keep customer details, credit decisions, sales, payments, and unpaid balances in one account.
Approve credit deliberately and give the customer clear limits and payment terms.

## Common actions

- [Creating a customer and setting credit](creating-a-customer-with-credit.md)
- [Making a credit sale](../selling/making-a-credit-sale.md)
- [Receiving a customer payment](receiving-a-customer-payment.md)

## Understand the balance

A credit sale increases the amount the customer owes. A customer payment reduces it. If a payment
is more than the open invoices, Dukarun keeps the extra amount as a downpayment for future use.
