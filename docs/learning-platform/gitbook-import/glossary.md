# Glossary

## Product

An item or family of items the business buys, stocks, or sells.

## Variant

A specific sellable version of a product, such as a size, colour, or pack size. Stock and barcodes
belong to the variant.

## Barcode

A machine-readable code used to identify one active product variant. A sellable barcode should be
unique.

## Inventory tracking

Recording how a variant's quantity changes through purchases, sales, adjustments, and transfers.

## Stock location

A physical shop, store, or warehouse where tracked inventory is held.

## Supplier

A person or organization from which the business purchases goods or services.

## Credit purchase

A purchase received now for which some or all payment is due to the supplier later.

## Payable

An amount the business owes a supplier.

## Customer

A person or organization that buys from the business and may have a tracked account.

## Credit limit

The configured maximum credit exposure approved for a customer or supplier relationship. In
Dukarun, zero may mean that no fixed cap is configured; approval remains separate.

## Credit sale

A completed sale for which the customer will pay some or all of the amount later.

## Receivable

An amount a customer owes the business.

## COGS

Cost of goods sold: the recorded stock cost attached to products that were sold in a period.

## Gross margin

Sales revenue less COGS, before other operating costs.

## Cash received

Money actually collected, which is different from revenue earned on credit.
