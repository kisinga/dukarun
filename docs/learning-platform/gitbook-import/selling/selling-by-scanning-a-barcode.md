# Selling by scanning a barcode

## Purpose

Add the correct product quickly with a connected scanner or the device camera.

## Prerequisites

The active product variant must have a unique barcode and be available for sale.

## Repeatable steps

1. Open **Sell** and focus **Search or scan barcode**.
2. Scan with a connected barcode scanner, or choose **Scan** to use the camera.
3. Confirm the correct product was added, then complete checkout.

## Expected result

A unique active barcode resolves to the correct variant and adds it to the cart.
