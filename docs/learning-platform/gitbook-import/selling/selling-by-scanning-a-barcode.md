# Selling by scanning a barcode

## Why this matters

Scanning is faster than searching and reduces the chance of adding the wrong product variant.

## Before you start

The variant and chosen selling unit must be active and available. A pack needs a selling price and
its own barcode. Test newly printed labels before using them during a busy shift.

## Steps

1. Open **Sell**.
2. To use a connected barcode scanner, place the cursor in **Search or scan barcode**, then scan.
3. To use the device camera, choose **Scan**, allow camera access, and hold the barcode inside the
   frame.
4. Check the variant, selling unit, quantity, and price. A pack barcode adds one whole pack; a base
   barcode adds the individual unit using the variant's normal quantity step.
5. Continue with the normal cash or credit checkout.

## What changes in Dukarun

The matching selling unit appears in the current sale. Scanning a tray barcode can add 30 eggs at
the tray price, while the individual-egg barcode adds an egg at its own price. Both use the same
stock balance. See [Setting up and using packs](../products/using-product-packs.md).

## If something does not look right

- If more than one variant matches, open **Products** and correct the duplicate barcode.
- If nothing happens, reconnect the scanner or allow camera access when prompted.
- If the wrong item appears, stop and correct the barcode before completing the sale.
- If the correct pack cannot be added, check that enough stock remains for all its contents.
- Newly created or changed pack barcodes need a catalogue refresh while connected before they can
  be used reliably offline. An incomplete offline catalogue cannot confirm a unique barcode.
