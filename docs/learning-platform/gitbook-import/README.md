# Dukarun Help

Find a task, understand the business result, then practise it in Dukarun. Search the knowledge hub
for an action or business term, or browse by the work you are doing.

## Learn the normal business cycle

Create a product and supplier, buy stock on credit, make cash and credit sales, then connect the
work to stock and financial results.

[Start or continue your first business cycle](https://app.dukarun.com/learn/first-business-cycle)

## Browse by business area

- [Products](products/README.md) — create, identify, price, and track what you sell.
- [Selling](selling/README.md) — find products and complete cash or credit sales.
- [Purchases](purchases/README.md) — receive stock and record how suppliers are paid.
- [Customers & Credit](customers-and-credit/README.md) — manage customer accounts and approval.
- [Suppliers](suppliers/README.md) — manage suppliers, terms, and amounts owed.
- [Money & Reporting](money-and-reporting/README.md) — explain cash, balances, revenue, cost, and margin.
