# Reviewing credit health

## Why this matters

Credit health shows money customers still owe, supplier bills still due, overdue balances, and
accounts that need attention.

## Before you start

Your role must allow you to view financial information. Make sure purchases, sales, and payments
have been recorded against the correct customer or supplier.

## Steps

1. Open **Money**, then **Credit**.
2. Review **Net receivables** to see what customers owe after account credits and adjustments.
3. Review **Supplier bills due by 7d** and total payables.
4. Use **Exposure by due status** to separate current, overdue, and older balances.
5. Check **Customer credit risk** for accounts using too much of their approved limit.
6. Use **Collect now** and **Pay soon** to decide which customer or supplier account to open next.
7. Open the account, verify the source invoices or purchases, then record the payment when money
   changes hands.

## What changes in Dukarun

Reviewing the screen does not change any balance. Recording a customer payment reduces receivables.
Recording a supplier payment reduces payables.

## If something does not look right

- An empty screen can be correct when there is no outstanding credit.
- If a balance is attached to the wrong party, correct the source record instead of creating an
  offsetting payment.
- Compare the account activity with the original invoice, purchase, and payment before acting on an
  unexpected balance.
