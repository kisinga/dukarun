# Dukarun route to market: first 10 paying businesses

## The immediate goal

Win 10 paying, owner-led businesses and learn whether they can reach a useful daily closing with
little support. Do not optimize for registrations. Optimize for paid activation and continued use.

Start with one narrow customer:

- One shop or location
- Owner plus one to three staff
- Regular cash and M-Pesa sales
- Stock, expenses or customer credit that the owner struggles to reconcile
- The owner can join the first 30-minute session

Do not actively target a new vertical until five interviews show that Dukarun already supports its
main daily workflow.

## The two offers

### Self-start

Use for a simple operation that can begin with five products or services.

- Customer pays the current introductory subscription offer.
- Dukarun provides a 30-minute orientation and self-help guides.
- Extra setup is not silently included.

### Assisted implementation

Use when the customer has existing data, several staff, multiple locations or a process that needs
mapping.

- Scope setup, data preparation and staff training before quoting.
- Quote implementation separately from the subscription.
- Typical implementation engagements are above KES 40,000; duration and staff count determine the
  final quote.
- Write down the deliverables, customer responsibilities, training dates and go-live date.

## Salesperson kit

Every salesperson receives only these assets:

1. A personal registration link: `APP_URL/register?sales_code=THEIR_CODE`
2. A QR code that opens the same link
3. The public cash-up tool: `SITE_URL/tools/daily-shop-cash-up`
4. The qualification questions below
5. A five-minute product demonstration

The salesperson must not promise custom features, free implementation or a price that is not on the
current offer.

## Qualification conversation

Ask before demonstrating:

1. How do you know what was sold today?
2. How do you compare those sales with cash and M-Pesa?
3. Do customers buy on credit?
4. Who handles the shop when the owner is away?
5. What mistake, shortage or stock problem happened recently?
6. How many staff, products and locations do you have?

Use the cash-up tool with the owner when closing control is the pain. Route a straightforward
operation to self-start. Route data-heavy or staff-heavy operations to an implementation call.

## The 30-minute orientation

- **0–5 minutes:** Create the business and confirm the normal payment methods.
- **5–12 minutes:** Add five frequently sold products or services.
- **12–20 minutes:** Record a real or sample sale.
- **20–25 minutes:** Record one expense or credit sale if relevant.
- **25–30 minutes:** Close the day, read the totals and agree on the next action.

Do not tour every menu. Success is one completed business cycle.

## Lead stages

Use [the first-10 tracker template](templates/first-10-leads.csv). A lead moves through:

`Identified → Owner contacted → Qualified → Demo completed → Paid → First sale → Active day 7 → Active day 14 → Renewed`

Every lost lead needs one reason: `price`, `no urgency`, `too complex`, `missing workflow`, `trust`,
`owner unavailable`, or `wrong segment`.

## Commission rule

Attribute the lead using the salesperson's registration link. A registration by itself earns
nothing. Treat collected payment as the first condition and successful activation as the quality
condition. Do not increase commission to compensate for weak qualification.

The product currently attributes the salesperson and supports commission on the first subscription
payment. Review activation and refund status before releasing a payout.

## Weekly review

Review these numbers every Monday:

- Qualified owners contacted
- Demonstrations completed
- Customers who paid
- Time from payment to first sale
- Active customers on days 7 and 14
- Human support minutes per self-start customer
- Renewals
- Lost reasons

For the first cohort, the working thresholds are:

- 7 of 10 complete a first sale within 30 minutes.
- 6 of 10 remain active in week two.
- Median support for self-start remains below 30 minutes.
- 6 of 10 renew after the introductory period.

When the same blocker appears for three customers, fix the copy, default, guide or workflow before
adding an unrelated feature.

## When to add training capacity

Do not hire a salaried trainer because labour is available. First record actual delivery hours.

- Use a paid contractor for scheduled implementation work while demand is uneven.
- Consider a dedicated hire only after training and implementation require at least 25 hours a week
  for four consecutive weeks.
- The booked implementation revenue should comfortably cover the person's full monthly cost before
  hiring. Use 1.5 times the full cost as the minimum safety margin.

This keeps training capacity tied to paid demand rather than registrations or optimistic forecasts.
