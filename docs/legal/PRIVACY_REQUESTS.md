# Privacy request procedure

Owner and counsel-approved response deadlines must be assigned before publication.

1. Record the request date, requester, contact channel, requested right and affected merchant.
2. Acknowledge without exposing whether an account or record exists.
3. Verify identity proportionately. For an agent, verify both the data subject and authority.
4. Decide whether Dukarun is controller or processor for the requested data. Processor requests are
   coordinated with the controlling merchant unless law requires a different response.
5. Search auth accounts, memberships/profiles, companies, customers/suppliers, sales/purchases,
   ledger/audit data, billing, storage objects, outbox/campaign history, support records and backups.
6. Check legal holds, accounting retention, fraud, security and third-party rights before changing
   or deleting anything. Record the basis for each withheld item.
7. Have the privacy owner approve the response and use an authenticated delivery channel.
8. Record completion, disclosures, corrections, restrictions, deletion actions and follow-up dates.

Requests go to the monitored `hello@dukarun.com` mailbox. Avoid asking
for identity documents unless necessary; delete verification copies under the approved schedule.
