# Privacy Notice

This notice explains how Dukarun handles personal data when merchants, staff, customers and visitors use the service.

## Who we are

Dukarun Solutions, referred to as Dukarun, we or us in this notice, operates the Dukarun service and is responsible for the processing described here.

Privacy questions and requests may be sent to hello@dukarun.com.

## Our data protection roles

Dukarun acts as a controller for account administration, subscriptions, security, support and service communications involving merchants and their staff.

A merchant normally decides why customer, supplier and staff data is entered into Dukarun. For that data, the merchant is the controller and Dukarun processes the data under the merchant's instructions.

## Data we handle

We may handle:

- Account and staff details such as names, phone numbers, roles, profile images and authentication records.
- Business details such as company information, locations, catalogues, inventory, suppliers and settings.
- Customer details entered by merchants, including names, contact details, balances, credit terms and communication preferences.
- Transaction and accounting records, including orders, payments, refunds, purchases, journals and audit events.
- Subscription information and Paystack payment references. We do not store full payment card details.
- Support, notification and message delivery records.
- Technical information needed for security, synchronization and reliable operation.

## Why we use data

We use data to:

- Create and manage accounts, companies, roles and subscriptions.
- Provide sales, inventory, accounting, credit and reporting features.
- Authenticate users by one-time code and protect the service from misuse.
- Send receipts, statements, reminders and merchant-authorized messages.
- Provide support and improve reliability.
- Meet legal, accounting, security and dispute obligations.

The legal basis depends on the context and may include performing a contract, complying with law, legitimate interests or consent where consent is required.

## Browser storage and offline use

Dukarun uses essential session storage, local preferences, service worker caches and IndexedDB. IndexedDB may hold company-scoped products, customers, suppliers, carts, recent sales, settings and unsynchronized transactions on the device.

Users should sign out on shared devices and protect their devices. Clearing browser data can remove local settings and unsynchronized work. We do not currently use advertising cookies or non-essential behavioral tracking.

## Sharing and service providers

We use service providers for infrastructure, network security, payments and message delivery. We may also disclose data to professional advisers, a successor operating the service, or public authorities where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data. Current service providers are listed in the Subprocessors document.

## International transfers

Some providers may process data outside Kenya. Where that happens, we use the safeguards required by Kenyan data protection law and our contracts with those providers.

## Retention

We keep personal data only while it is needed to provide the service, meet legal and accounting duties, maintain security, resolve disputes or exercise legal rights.

Closing an account does not automatically erase records that must be retained for accounting, fraud prevention, disputes or other legal obligations. We delete or de-identify data when there is no longer a valid reason to keep it.

## Security

We use one-time-code authentication, role and tenant access controls, database row policies, encrypted network transport, scoped offline caches, audit records and managed backups.

No system is completely secure. Users remain responsible for protecting their devices, login sessions and authorized staff access.

## Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, a person may ask for access, correction, restriction, objection or deletion. A merchant's customer should normally contact that merchant first because the merchant controls the business relationship.

Send requests to hello@dukarun.com. We will verify identity and authority, coordinate with the relevant merchant where needed, and explain any lawful reason for retaining or restricting records. A person may also complain to Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

## Children

Dukarun business accounts are not intended for children. A person must be at least 18 and authorized by a business to create or administer a company workspace.

## Changes to this notice

We publish the current version and effective date with this notice. We notify account administrators of material changes through the service or available contact channels.
