# Data Processing Addendum

This Data Processing Addendum forms part of the Dukarun Terms of Service when Dukarun processes personal data for a merchant.

## Parties and roles

This addendum applies between Dukarun Solutions and the merchant. The merchant normally acts as controller for personal data it enters about staff, customers, suppliers and other business contacts. Dukarun acts as processor for that data.

Dukarun remains a controller for its own account administration, subscription, security and legal compliance activities.

## Processing instructions

Dukarun processes merchant personal data only to provide, secure and support the service under the Terms, product configuration, support requests and lawful written instructions.

Processing may cover:

- People such as merchant staff, customers, suppliers and contacts appearing in transaction or support records.
- Identity, contact, role, sales, credit, payment reference, communication, device and audit information.
- Hosting, authentication, point of sale, accounting, support, security, backup and merchant-requested communications.
- The service relationship and the period needed for approved backup, legal and deletion requirements.

## Dukarun's duties

Dukarun will:

- Bind authorized personnel to confidentiality.
- Limit access according to role and operational need.
- Maintain appropriate technical and organizational security measures.
- Assist with rights requests, assessments and regulator consultations where reasonably required.
- Notify the merchant of a relevant personal data breach without undue delay and provide available facts.
- Delete, return or lawfully retain data at the end of service.
- Provide information reasonably needed to demonstrate compliance.

## Subprocessors and transfers

Dukarun may use subprocessors to provide infrastructure, payment and communication services. We require appropriate data protection and confidentiality commitments from subprocessors.

The current Subprocessors document identifies providers and their purposes. We will give reasonable notice of a material addition where required and consider substantiated data protection objections.

Cross-border processing will use safeguards required by Kenyan data protection law.

## Merchant duties

The merchant will:

- Provide required notices and establish a lawful basis for processing.
- Configure access, communications and retention appropriately.
- Avoid entering unnecessary or unlawfully obtained data.
- Keep documented instructions lawful.
- Respond to its data subjects and give Dukarun enough verified information to assist.

## Security and incidents

Dukarun uses tenant isolation, role-based access, one-time-code authentication, encrypted network transport, audit records, backups and scoped offline storage.

Each party will provide information reasonably needed to assess and respond to an incident affecting merchant personal data.

## Return and deletion

On account closure, Dukarun will support the available export and deletion process. Data may be retained where required for accounting, security, fraud prevention, legal claims or another lawful purpose. Remaining data will be deleted or de-identified when that purpose ends.

## Order of documents

If this addendum conflicts with the Terms on the processing of merchant personal data, this addendum controls for that issue. The rest of the Terms continues to apply.
