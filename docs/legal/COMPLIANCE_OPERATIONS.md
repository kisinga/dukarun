# Compliance operations

This internal runbook covers provider review, retention, regulatory records and account closure.
It is not published in the applications.

## Providers and international transfers

Before sending personal data to a provider, record:

- the provider's legal name and service;
- the data involved and why it is needed;
- where the data is processed;
- its role as controller or processor;
- security, breach, deletion and audit terms; and
- the contract or transfer safeguard relied on.

Review active providers each year and before a material service change. Keep the public
subprocessor document in sync with approved providers.

## ODPC records

Keep the current assessment of whether Dukarun acts as a controller, processor or both. Retain any
applications, certificates, renewal dates and updates. Review the assessment after a material change
to the product, company, processing activities or applicable law.

## Retention

Maintain an approved retention register for account data, merchant records, transactions, audit
logs, messages, media, support records, incidents, backups and offline browser data. For each
category, record its purpose, retention period, owner and final action.

Do not delete records that must be retained for accounting, tax, fraud prevention, security, a
legal hold or an active dispute. Restrict access when full deletion is not yet allowed. Review the
register each year and record evidence of completed deletion or anonymisation.

## Manual export and account closure

1. Accept the request from a verified company administrator through the privacy mailbox.
2. Resolve unsynchronised device activity and pause destructive changes.
3. Export the available company, profile, catalogue, customer, transaction, ledger and message data.
4. Obtain Privacy and Finance approval for the closure.
5. Disable access and revoke public storefront and statement links.
6. Cancel provider subscriptions and delete data that is no longer required.
7. Restrict retained accounting, audit, security and dispute records for the approved period.
8. Attach completion evidence to the request record.
