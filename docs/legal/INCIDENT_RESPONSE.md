# Personal-data incident response

## Immediate response

1. Open an incident record and preserve timestamps, logs and affected versions.
2. Contain access without destroying evidence; rotate credentials or isolate services as needed.
3. Identify affected systems, companies, data categories, subjects, countries and likely harm.
4. Notify the security, privacy, infrastructure and executive owners. Involve counsel early.
5. Determine whether Dukarun is controller, processor or both for each affected dataset.

## Notification assessment

- Start the ODPC assessment clock when the breach is discovered; document any uncertainty.
- Where Dukarun is processor, notify the controlling merchant without undue delay with available
  facts and ongoing updates.
- Counsel decides whether ODPC notification is required and prepares it within the applicable
  72-hour window, or documents reasons for delay.
- Notify affected people when required, in clear language, with protective steps and a contact.
- Coordinate provider, insurer, law-enforcement and contractual notifications without delaying
  legally required notices.

## Closure

Confirm containment, recovery, synchronization integrity and credential rotation. Record root cause,
scope, decisions, notifications and corrective actions. Track remediation to completion and update
the risk assessment, runbooks, provider review and training.
